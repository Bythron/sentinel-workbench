# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss technical ideas without personal attacks, harassment, threats or discriminatory language. Protect private data in issues, logs and examples. Security concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue threads. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to keep the community safe. Report conduct concerns privately to maintainers through a private channel once one is published; until then, do not include personal data in public reports.
